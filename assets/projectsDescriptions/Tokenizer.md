# The Tokenizer: Rise of the Ledgers

A retro 2D platformer game chronicling the journey of tokenization from the 2017 Wild West era to the historic 2025 Wall Street public announcement.

## Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Or use the provided scripts:
   - Windows: `start.bat`
   - Unix/Linux/Mac: `start.sh`

## Game Overview

Play as "The Tokenizer" representing Securitize's journey through 6 pivotal eras of tokenization history. Battle FUD, overcome regulatory challenges, and witness the evolution of digital securities.

### Win Condition
- Collect all 15 SPiCE tokens AND defeat all enemies in each level
- Complete all 6 levels to witness Securitize's triumphant Wall Street debut

### Controls
- **Movement**: A/D or Arrow Keys
- **Jump**: W or Up Arrow (hold for higher jumps)
- **Shoot**: Space or Z
- **Pause**: P

## Features

- 6 unique levels with different themes and challenges
- 12+ enemy types with unique behaviors and animations
- Boss battles with special mechanics
- Collectible system with premium coins and power-ups
- Lives system that persists across levels
- 75-second timer per level
- Comprehensive SFX system with 17+ sound effects
- Music system with level-appropriate tracks
- Advanced scoring with combos and precision bonuses
- Paginated leaderboard (25 scores per page)

- Vanilla JavaScript (ES6 modules)

- CSS3 for UI styling
- Web Audio API for sound effects and music
