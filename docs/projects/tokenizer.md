## Overview

Play as "The Tokenizer," battling through 6 pivotal eras of crypto-tokenization history — FUD, regulatory hurdles, and everything in between — on the way to a Wall Street public announcement. It's a fully playable, vanilla-JS retro platform shooter with boss battles, a global leaderboard, and a comprehensive SFX/music system, built to make a piece of industry history genuinely fun to experience rather than just read about.

## Goals

- Turn an abstract, multi-year industry narrative (tokenization's evolution 2017–2025) into something people actually want to play through.
- Build a full retro platformer — levels, enemies, bosses, scoring, leaderboard — using nothing but vanilla JS and HTML5 Canvas, no game engine.
- Make it reliable to load and share: zero broken sprites, zero 404s, and proper social-sharing metadata.

## Key Features

- 6 unique levels, each themed around a different era of tokenization history, with 12+ enemy types and boss battles.
- Collectible system (15 SPiCE tokens per level) plus power-ups and a persistent lives system.
- 75-second timer per level with a combo/precision scoring system.
- 17+ sound effects and level-appropriate music tracks via the Web Audio API.
- Paginated global leaderboard (25 scores per page) with security hardening.
- Real-time loading progress that pre-loads all 200+ assets before gameplay starts — no broken sprites, no mid-game asset pop-in.

## Potential Use Cases

- A creative, memorable way to communicate an industry story (crypto-tokenization's timeline) at a conference booth, on a company site, or in a portfolio.
- A reference build for anyone learning vanilla-JS/Canvas game development without a framework.

## How to Play

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or run `start.bat` (Windows) / `start.sh` (macOS/Linux). Or just play it live at [thetokenizer.online](https://thetokenizer.online/).

**Controls:** A/D or arrow keys to move, W/Up to jump (hold for higher jumps), Space/Z to shoot, P to pause.

**Win condition:** collect all 15 SPiCE tokens and defeat all enemies in each level; complete all 6 levels to reach the Wall Street debut ending.

## Tech Stack

Vanilla JavaScript (ES6 modules), HTML5 Canvas for rendering, CSS3 for UI, Web Audio API for sound and music. No game engine, no build step.
